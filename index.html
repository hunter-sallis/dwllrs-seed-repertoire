Musical fingerprint — signal analysis, not listening

librosa 1.0.0 on the supplied 44.1 kHz/16-bit stereo WAV (decoded from a 128 kbps source per the filename), analysed mono at 22,050 Hz; ffmpeg ebur128 for loudness. No human or model listening took place: every row below is a signal statistic or an attributed description.

Tempo

103 BPM. Beat tracker 103.4; frame-wise tempogram mode 103 (4,480 frames vs 815 for the runner-up 123). Shazam also lists 103 BPM. Half/double-time readings (51.5 / 206) are not supported by the tempogram.

Key

Unresolved between two readings. Krumhansl–Schmuckler on mean chroma ranks C♯ minor (r = 0.71) ahead of C♯ major (0.62); chroma peaks are G♯, C♯, A, F♯, E. A community chord chart (Ultimate Guitar, user noah.paxman53, standard tuning, no capo) gives a D–F♯m–A–E loop, which is diatonic to A major / F♯ minor. The chart's D chord is weak in the chroma (0.35), so the recording's tonal centre should be confirmed by ear before it is quoted.

Loudness

Integrated −7.4 LUFS, loudness range 5.2 LU, true peak +0.2 dBTP: a loud, compressed modern pop master, not a quiet acoustic recording.

Texture

Percussive-to-harmonic energy ratio stays low (0.23–0.32) and onset density falls from ~2/s in the first minute to 0.2–0.4/s after 1:20. The signal is sustained-chord and vocal dominated rather than drum-forward; sharp transients are sparse once the full arrangement is in.

Stereo

Left/right correlation 0.53 — a moderately wide mix.

Lyrical perspective & hook

Not reproduced. Perspective per the Shazam lyric page and the community chord chart: first-person verses about restlessness, time passing and friends moving on; a second-person pre-chorus about getting lost in the dark on the way to someone's heart; verse 2 and the bridge turn to Jesus/Christ 'turning the lights back on' and an invitation into the light. The chorus repeats the title line eight times; the bridge repeats its own four-word mantra and interlocks with the final chorus. YouTube tags added by the channel include 'jesus turned the lights back on'.

Methodology, sources and limits

Method

  1. Seed identity from Apple Music, Spotify (signed-out pages and public embed JSON), YouTube metadata and Chartmetric; four versions catalogued and kept apart.
  2. Fingerprint from librosa and ffmpeg on the supplied WAV; structure inferred against a community chord chart; no listening.
  3. CSV audit reproduced the supplied counts exactly; raw rows preserved; repeated pairs treated as possible repeat placements.
  4. Playlist resolution by name + curator via Chartmetric (top 12) and Spotify search (small lists); contents from the public embed.
  5. Candidate pool from Shazam similar songs (whose IDs double as Apple Music song IDs), Apple and Chartmetric similar artists, co-occurrence and adjacency, name clues and reciprocal lists.
  6. Verification of artist, exact version and release date per recording via Spotify embed data and the iTunes Search API; identities from Spotify About text and press.
  7. Ranking by the four-dimension rubric with evidence confidence held separate.

Material limits

  • No listening. Sonic and vocal judgments are capped at Medium/Unassessed for every candidate, and the seed fingerprint is signal statistics aligned to a community chord chart.
  • Playlist contents come from Spotify's public embed, which exposes only the first 100 tracks. Any placement beyond #100 (e.g., *JESUS* at #534, granola girl at #266) is resolved but not verified for co-occurrence.
  • The export has no playlist IDs; identities were resolved by name + curator through Chartmetric (top 12) or Spotify search (small lists). Six names remain unresolved.
  • Shazam blocked requests after about 25 pages, so BPM/label data covers 10 of 27 accepted recordings.
  • Chartmetric similar-artist and comparable-track tables were read for page 1 only (Chrome extension disconnected before pagination).
  • The '28-Day Change' column is a playlist-level Chartmetric field whose exact definition was not verified in-app; it is not song stream growth.
  • Playlist follower counts are export snapshots and were never summed as an audience figure.
  • Metrics are dated per platform and never combined across versions; the second Spotify single listing (35dPLCeXcr0ZuRAhOdS0a1) was not merged with the seed.
